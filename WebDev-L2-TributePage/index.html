Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Scientist, teacher and the People's President

A life built around learning

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was one of India's best-known scientists and public figures. He played an important role in India's space and defence programmes before becoming the country's 11th President.

What made Kalam especially popular was his simple way of speaking and his strong belief in education. He spent a lot of time talking with students and encouraging young people to set goals and work patiently towards them.

Even after his presidency, he continued teaching, writing and meeting students. His public life is remembered for science, education, humility and a strong interest in India's future.

Some important years

1931

Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.

1960

Joined India's space research programme after completing his studies in aerospace engineering.

1980

Worked with the team behind the successful launch of the Rohini satellite using the SLV-III.

1998

Played a leading role in India's Pokhran-II nuclear tests.

2002

Became the 11th President of India.

2015

Died while delivering a lecture to students in Shillong.
